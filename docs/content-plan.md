# Content Plan & Keyword Map: Matrix of Destiny

## 1. Competitor Analysis

### Competitors Audited
1. **thematrixofdestiny.com**
   - **Topics Covered:** Basic chart generator, brief 22 Arcana summaries, karmic tail list.
   - **Search Intents Served:** Broad informational queries ("what is matrix of destiny"), transactional calculator runs.
   - **Gaps:** Super-short generic texts (100–200 words), zero position-specific breakdowns, no formula derivations.
   - **Risky/Weak Claims:** Fatalistic descriptions of karmic debts; promises guaranteed personality prediction.
2. **matrixdestinycalculator.com**
   - **Topics Covered:** Calculator, 22 major arcana profiles, compatibility calculator, love/money line descriptions.
   - **Search Intents Served:** High-volume calculator searches, quick keyword lookups.
   - **Gaps:** Ignores why different tools calculate different numbers; lacks balanced vs shadow behavioral tables.
   - **Risky/Weak Claims:** Diagnoses health conditions and physical ailments from the chakra table (medical risk).
3. **matrix-destiny.org**
   - **Topics Covered:** Octagram geometry, brief Russian methodology background, basic zone tables.
   - **Search Intents Served:** Informational definitions, basic chart diagram explanations.
   - **Gaps:** Poor English translation quality, outdated static layouts, no practical reflection exercises.
   - **Risky/Weak Claims:** Claims ancient secret lineage dating back thousands of years without historical evidence.
4. **datemyst.com**
   - **Topics Covered:** Aesthetic charts, relationship compatibility scores, paid downloadable PDF blueprints.
   - **Search Intents Served:** Commercial/transactional queries for personalized chart reports.
   - **Gaps:** Heavily paywalled; locks essential calculation formulas and position meanings behind paid barriers.
   - **Risky/Weak Claims:** Claims soulmate compatibility percentages can predict relationship success or failure.
5. **avatariumlife.com**
   - **Topics Covered:** 22 Arcana career paths, financial codes, yearly forecast intro.
   - **Search Intents Served:** Career advice, yearly forecasts.
   - **Gaps:** Confuses yearly forecast calculation methods; does not explain the 3 decomposed transit layers.
   - **Risky/Weak Claims:** Guarantees specific financial windfall dates based on personal year numbers.

### Our Differentiating Value Proposition
- **Architectural & Psychological Clarity:** Treat the 22 Arcana as archetypal mirrors and behavioral patterns, not fatalistic fortune-telling.
- **Transparent Calculations:** Full step-by-step arithmetic formulas (modulo-22, digit summing) with worked examples.
- **Honest & Safe E-E-A-T Boundaries:** Explicit disclaimers that the method has no scientific or medical validation; chakra tables are used strictly for emotional and mindful reflection.
- **Position-Specific Depth:** Clear interpretations for the real engine positions calculated in `calculator.ts`.
